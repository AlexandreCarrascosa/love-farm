## Fotografia de miosótis

`forget-me-not.jpg` é uma cópia local de [“Woodland Forget-me-not (Myosotis sylvatica), close shot.jpg”](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Woodland_Forget-me-not_(Myosotis_sylvatica),_close_shot.jpg), foto de **Esaox**, disponibilizada sob [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). O autor dedicou a imagem ao domínio público; foi usada uma versão de 960 × 960 pixels do Wikimedia Commons. `dialog-branch.svg` foi desenhado para este projeto.

Geradas com a ferramenta integrada **ImageGen**, em 9 de outubro de 2026. Os PNGs originais foram preservados na pasta de imagens geradas do ambiente. As cópias deste projeto foram convertidas para WebP com qualidade 93%, preservando o canal alfa da nuvem. Não há carregamento externo.

- `assets/farm-aerial.webp`: cenário, 1575 × 998 pixels.
- `assets/cloud.webp`: nuvem, 1536 × 1024 pixels, com transparência verificada.

## Prompt do terreno

Create a production-ready background texture for a top-down 2D farm game. Output a wide landscape image, aspect 1200:760 (approximately 3:2). Camera points STRAIGHT DOWN, true orthographic aerial drone view, absolutely NO horizon, NO sky, NO perspective/isometric walls. Photorealistic natural miniature countryside, realistic detailed grass, earth, moss, mature tree canopies, gravel paths, warm late-afternoon sunlight from upper left and coherent cast shadows to lower right. Calm romantic spring atmosphere. Strict composition to support gameplay overlays: upper 35% is mostly open textured deep green meadow with a few tree clusters near edges; center-left at normalized x .17 y .55 a rustic farmhouse seen ONLY as a weathered terracotta roof rectangular footprint .18 image width and .17 image height, with patio and natural stone garden. A narrow small rustic dirt airstrip horizontal at x .12-.34, y .36-.40, completely empty. Lower right at x .322-.855, y .558-.816 is a perfectly rectangular cultivated farm plot, flat bare dark rich earth with faint plow lines; EMPTY of plants, vehicles and writing, because the game will draw its own crops there. There is a dirt lane surrounding this field. Bottom-left at x .07-.20 y .77-.89 is a small separate bed of very small blue forget-me-not flowers. A small natural dark teal pond with reeds and stones near x .92 y .76, outside the main plot. A sparse orchard of leafy green trees in the far upper-left, around the farmhouse, and along the far right edge; all canopies from directly above with intricate realistic leaves. White timber fences border some paths. Large natural grass negative spaces surround objects for free character movement. No people, no tractor, no airplane, no clouds (clouds are separate animated layers), no text, no interface, no letters, no hearts, no watermark. Natural lush green and warm earth palette, detailed realistic atmospheric photography, NOT cartoon, NOT vector, NOT pixel art, NOT a painting. Cohesive premium cozy farming-game world.

## Prompt da nuvem

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: transparent PNG cloud sprite for a realistic top-down aerial farm game. Generate ONE isolated natural small cumulus cloud, viewed from directly above by a drone or aircraft flying higher than the cloud. Wide irregular horizontal silhouette, detailed billowing bright ivory white lobes, dense rounded opaque sunlit centers, gentle realistic cool gray self-shadowing in crevices, delicate wispy translucent edges. Natural sunlight from upper left. Broad cloud cluster filling central 80% with generous transparent padding around all edges. True alpha transparent background, NO sky, NO land, NO horizon, NO frame, NO drop shadow, NO text. Photorealistic atmospheric cloud photography, volumetric and three dimensional. Avoid flat cartoon outlines, avoid generic Gaussian mist blob, avoid a rectangular background or checkerboard painted into the image. Landscape composition about 3:2.

A câmera foi recalibrada para a composição da imagem gerada. Original PNG preservado na pasta de imagens geradas do ambiente; versão WebP local com qualidade 93%.

Place the ENTIRE original rectangular reference in the EXACT CENTER occupying 50% of final image WIDTH and 31.67% of final image HEIGHT (its horizontal bounds 25%-75%, vertical bounds 34.165%-65.835%). Preserve the farmhouse, airstrip, empty rectangular plowed field, blue flower bed, pond and all their proportions/relative locations in this center. Extend the realistic environment seamlessly on all FOUR sides: lush varied meadows, natural irregular groves and forest clearings, continuation of the existing paths and fences, an organically extended small pond/stream to the right, shrubs, wildflower patches and textured earth. No new houses, no new cultivated rectangular fields, no vehicles. The enlarged landscape must be ONE continuous coherent aerial photograph, no visible rectangle boundary, no mirrored or tiled texture, no bands, no blank space, no seams, no blur fill. Camera straight down, identical scale, natural realistic texture, bright neutral daylight with gentle shadows. Outer areas should be rich in unique natural features but calm, no repeated symmetric patterns. This is an expanded video-game map with all sides visible on desktop ultrawide and phone portrait. No sky, no clouds, no interface, no words or letters, no black bars. The goal is zooming out from the existing farm to reveal much more surrounding nature while keeping the original farm at center.
